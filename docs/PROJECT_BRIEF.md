# Žemaitija Heat & Home

## Project Type
Portfolio case study / simulated commercial client project.

This is a fictional company created for portfolio purposes. The website should be designed and developed to the same standard as a genuine paying-client project.

## Business

Žemaitija Heat & Home is a small heating and plumbing company serving residential customers around Šiauliai and the wider Samogitia region of Lithuania.

The company has six engineers and provides:

- Heating system installation and repair
- Heat pump installation
- General plumbing
- Boiler and heating maintenance
- Emergency repairs
- Residential call-outs

Most existing work comes through referrals.

The business wants to generate more enquiries online and improve its professional image.

## Business Objectives

The website should:

1. Generate qualified customer enquiries.
2. Establish trust with homeowners.
3. Perform well on mobile devices.
4. Explain services clearly.
5. Target local Google searches.
6. Make contacting the company easy.
7. Support both Lithuanian and English customers.
8. Load quickly even on slower mobile connections.

## Target Customers

Primary customers:

- Homeowners
- Property owners
- Landlords
- Rural homeowners
- People renovating properties
- Customers considering heat pumps
- Customers requiring heating or plumbing repairs

Geographic focus:

- Šiauliai
- Surrounding towns and villages
- Approximately 50 km service radius

## Required Pages

### Home
Overview of the business, main services, trust signals and strong enquiry call-to-action.

### Heating
Heating installation, repair, maintenance and upgrades.

### Heat Pumps
Heat pump installation, benefits, suitability and quotation process.

### Plumbing
General domestic plumbing services.

### Emergency Repairs
Urgent plumbing and heating repairs with prominent contact options.

### Service Area
Areas served and local coverage.

### About
Company background, experience, working principles and customer reassurance.

### Contact
Contact details and enquiry form.

- Main service cards
- Heat pump section
- Reasons to choose the company
- Simple service process
- Service-area information
- Customer enquiry section

## Design Direction

The site should feel:

- Professional
- Reliable
- Modern
- Practical
- Local
- Established

Avoid:

- Overly corporate styling
- Excessive animation
- Generic startup appearance
- AI-looking design
- Large amounts of unnecessary text

The intended customer should immediately understand:

1. What the company does
2. Where it operates
3. Why it can be trusted
4. How to contact it

## Responsive Design

The website must work correctly on:

- Mobile phones
- Tablets
- Laptops
- Desktop displays

Mobile-first design should be prioritised.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.1 AA good practice.

Requirements include:

- Semantic HTML
- Keyboard navigation
- Appropriate colour contrast
- Form labels
- Alternative text for meaningful images
- Visible focus states
- Logical heading structure

## Performance

Target Lighthouse scores:

- Performance: 95+
- Accessibility: 95+
- Best Practices: 95+
- SEO: 95+

Avoid unnecessary JavaScript and oversized assets.

## SEO

Implement:

- Unique page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Semantic headings
- Local business structured data
- Service-specific content

- Sitemap

Final portfolio version should support:

- Lithuanian

Lithuanian should eventually be the primary customer-facing language.
